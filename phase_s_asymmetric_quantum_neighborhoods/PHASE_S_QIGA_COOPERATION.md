## 1. Algorithmic Distinction & Status

**Scientific Definition:**  
The Quantum-Inspired Genetic Algorithm (QIGA / IQGA) is a **purely classical metaheuristic** running on conventional digital hardware. It uses probability vectors called "Q-bits" ($[\alpha, \beta]^T$) to maintain a probability distribution over solution strings, updated via trigonometric rotation gates:
$$R(\Delta \theta) = \begin{bmatrix} \cos(\Delta \theta) & -\sin(\Delta \theta) \\ \sin(\Delta \theta) & \cos(\Delta \theta) \end{bmatrix}$$
It does **NOT** utilize physical quantum phenomena, quantum entanglement, or quantum coprocessors.

## 2. Experimental Cooperation Architectures

We evaluated six configurations across the 20 asymmetric instances:

| Configuration | Architectural Structure | Mean Cost (km) | Optimality Gap | Mean Runtime (ms) |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Config A: QAOA Alone** | Standalone V-QAOA with XY mixer & Hungarian matching. | 72.19 km | 20.72% | 618.9 ms |
| **Config B: QIGA Alone** | Classical Q-bit rotation search (pop=12, gen=20). | **61.56 km** | **0.00%** | **12.4 ms** |
| **Config C: QIGA-Guided QAOA** | QIGA finds candidate permutations; QAOA evaluates core bottleneck. | **61.56 km** | **0.00%** | 631.3 ms |

| **Classical Reference: VND** | Variable Neighborhood Descent (deterministic local search). | 61.77 km | 0.33% | **0.19 ms** |

## 3. Findings on Algorithmic Cooperation

1. **QIGA Compensates for QAOA Noise:**  
   Whenever QIGA is added to QAOA (Configs C, D, E), the optimality gap drops from **$20.72\%$ to $0.00\%$**. QIGA's probabilistic exploration resolves the suboptimal measurements generated by QAOA.
2. **The "Free-Rider" Effect:**  
   The $0.00\%$ optimality gap in Config C and D is achieved **entirely by QIGA, not by QAOA**. QIGA alone achieves the exact same $61.56\text{ km}$ in $12.4\text{ ms}$. Coupling QIGA with QAOA simply increases execution time by **$50\times$** ($12.4\text{ ms} \to 631.3\text{ ms}$) without altering the solution quality.
3. **Classical Local Search Comparison:**  
   While QIGA achieves $0.00\%$ gap in $12.4\text{ ms}$, deterministic classical Variable Neighborhood Descent (VND) reaches a $0.33\%$ gap in **$0.19\text{ ms}$**—over **$65\times$ faster** than QIGA.

---

## 4. Conclusion

QIGA is an effective classical stochastic diversifier, but claiming "quantum-classical synergy" when pairing QIGA with QAOA is scientifically misleading. The improvements observed in hybrid pipelines originate entirely from the classical Q-bit rotation search, while QAOA adds latency overhead.
