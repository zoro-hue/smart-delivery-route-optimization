## 1. FORMULATION ARCHITECTURE AUDIT

We audited the physical parameters, mathematical definitions, and circuit compilation of the best-performing QAOA formulations evaluated in Phases Y and Z:

### 1. Cost Hamiltonian ($H_C$)
For two vehicle clusters $A$ and $B$, the boundary customer bipartitioning problem maps $N$ customer nodes into Pauli-Z operators:
$$H_C = \sum_{i=1}^N h_i Z_i + \sum_{1 \le i < j \le N} J_{ij} Z_i Z_j$$
where:
$$h_i = -\frac{1}{2}(d_{iA} - d_{iB}) - \lambda_c (C_A - C_B - \frac{1}{2}\sum q_k) q_i$$
$$J_{ij} = -\frac{1}{2} W_{ij} + \frac{1}{2}\lambda_c q_i q_j$$
- **Interaction Strength ($W_{ij}$):** Modeled as $W_{ij} = \frac{5.0}{d_{ij} + 0.5}$, penalizing the separation of geographically proximate customers into different vehicle routes.
- **Penalty Coefficient ($\lambda_c$):** Set to $\lambda_c = 2.0$.

### 2. Mixer Hamiltonians ($H_M$)
- **Transverse Mixer (Formulation A):**
  $$H_M^{(X)} = \sum_{i=1}^N X_i$$
  Generates uniform superposition across all $2^N$ computational basis states.
- **Continuous XY-Ring Mixer (Formulation B):**
  $$H_M^{(XY)} = \frac{1}{2}\sum_{i=1}^N (X_i X_{i+1} + Y_i Y_{i+1})$$
  Restricts state dynamics to the subspace of constant Hamming weight $\sum x_i = N/2$.

## 2. LANDSCAPE MISALIGNMENT: QUBO CUT VS. TSP TOUR

A fundamental finding of Phase AA is the **surrogate objective disconnect**:

```
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| QUBO Surrogate Objective:                                                                        |
| Min Cut: sum(h_i x_i) + sum(J_ij x_i x_j)                                                        |
| Evaluates static centroid proximity and pairwise customer separation.                            |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                        VS.
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Complete Route Objective:                                                                        |
| TSP Length: sum_{e in Tour_A} c(e) + sum_{e in Tour_B} c(e)                                      |
| Evaluates sequential node ordering and street traversal permutations.                            |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Empirical Demonstration of Misalignment
In our benchmark across 20 instances:
- The **Exact Global Optimum of the QUBO Hamiltonian** (Variant 5) produced a complete fleet route distance of **$581.96\text{ km}$**.
- Meanwhile, Fast QAOA (which sampled slightly suboptimal QUBO states with energies $\sim 5\% - 10\%$ above the ground state) produced **$575.74\text{ km}$**!
- **Scientific Implication:** Finding the true quantum ground state of $H_C$ does NOT necessarily minimize delivery route distance. A partition that has higher QUBO cut cost can possess better spatial convexity for TSP sequencing, allowing VND to build a shorter physical delivery tour.

## 3. COMPARISON OF SAMPLING DISTRIBUTIONS

We compared the empirical bitstring sampling distributions across 4 generators:

| Generator | Search Mechanism | Exploration Bias | Feasible Partition Rate | Complete Route Distance (km) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Uniform Random** | Flat unbiased sampling | None (Pure exploration) | 61.2% | 576.40 |
| **Simulated Annealing** | Metropolis thermal walks | Local energy gradients | 78.4% | 603.70 |
| **Tabu Search** | Memory-restricted local moves | Steepest descent + recency | **92.1%** | **582.61** |
| **Subspace XY-QAOA** | Quantum dispersion in Hamming subspace | Unitary interference | 79.9% | 575.74 |

### Conclusion
The quantum statevector distribution generated by QAOA does not locate hidden non-local structures that classical search cannot reach. Rather, its primary function is acting as a **moderately biased, diversified candidate generator** whose outputs become viable only when filtered through classical capacity repair and Proposition 1 incumbent acceptance.
