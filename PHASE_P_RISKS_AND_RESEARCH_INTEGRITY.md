## 1. Confronting Uncomfortable Scientific Realities

To maintain submission-grade journal integrity, the ROUTE/IQ project must rigorously confront the practical limitations of near-term quantum computing and acknowledge known failure modes before peer reviewers point them out.

### Reality 1: The Enumeration Paradox on Micro-Clusters (\(K = 4\))
- For a subproblem of \(K = 4\) customers, the feasible permutation space contains \(4! = 24\) permutations.
- A single classical CPU core evaluates all 24 permutations in **\(0.0008\text{ ms}\)** using simple brute-force enumeration.
- Simulating a 16-qubit QAOA circuit on Qiskit Aer takes **\(120\text{ to }250\text{ ms}\)**—over **150,000 times slower** than classical enumeration!
- **Scientific Justification:** QAOA is evaluated here as an **algorithmic prototype for future fault-tolerant quantum hardware**, investigating whether variational quantum interference can navigate discrete combinatorial energy landscapes. We must **never** claim a computational speedup on classical simulators for \(K=4\).

---

### Reality 2: The Warm-Start Collapse Risk
- If classical warm-starting is made too strong (e.g. setting \(\beta \to 0\) or heavily damping the mixer), QAOA will simply measure the starting classical permutation with 99.9% probability.
- If this occurs, QAOA is merely acting as an expensive classical identity operator!
- **Mitigation & Experimental Check:** The experimental harness must measure the **Quantum Exploration Entropy** \(S_Q = -\sum p_i \log p_i\) and record the fraction of non-classical candidate permutations generated by QAOA. If \(S_Q \approx 0\), the mixer angle \(\beta\) must be tuned upward to permit quantum tunneling.

---

### Reality 3: The Threat of Superior Classical Baselines
- Modern industrial CVRP solvers (Google OR-Tools using Guided Local Search, Large Neighborhood Search, and tabu lists) have benefited from 40+ years of classical optimization research.
- On standard planar benchmark instances, OR-Tools frequently achieves within 0.5% of the theoretical global optimum.
- It is statistically probable that across 90 experimental runs, OR-Tools alone will match or exceed the hybrid quantum solution on 85–95% of instances.
- **Journal Stance on Negative Results:** If the classical baselines match or outperform quantum-assisted methods, **ROUTE/IQ will report this honestly as a primary scientific finding**. Documenting the precise empirical boundaries where quantum assistance fails to improve over state-of-the-art classical operations research is of immense scientific value to the IEEE/ACM literature, actively correcting unfounded "quantum supremacy" hype in supply chain logistics.

## 2. Mandatory Attribution & Publication Commitments

In all Phase P manuscripts, reports, and API documentation:

1. **Explicit Classical Simulation Disclosure:**
   - Always state: *"Quantum circuits were simulated classically using the Qiskit Aer statevector simulator (`AerSimulator`) on x86_64 CPU hardware."*
   - Never use ambiguous phrasing like *"executed on our quantum processor"* or *"run on a quantum node"*.
2. **Strict Prohibition of "Quantum Supremacy" Claims:**
   - Prohibit claims of "quantum supremacy", "quantum advantage", or "exponential quantum speedup".
   - Use verified terminology: *"quantum-assisted heuristic"*, *"variational candidate exploration"*, *"cooperative hybrid pipeline"*.
3. **Disclosure of Proposition 1 Guardrail Mechanics:**
   - When reporting that the hybrid pipeline never degrades below classical solution quality, explicitly disclose that this is guaranteed by the **algorithmic incumbent rejection filter**, not by an inherent physical property of the quantum circuit.
4. **Reproducibility Guarantee:**
   - All source code, random seeds, benchmark road graphs, and raw JSON execution logs must be deposited in open-access scientific repositories (Zenodo / GitHub) with DOIs upon manuscript acceptance.
